Why Invest in Zirakpur

A Corridor on the Rise

Zirakpur has grown from a quiet junction town into one of the Tricity's fastest-appreciating residential corridors, sitting at the crossroads of Chandigarh, Mohali, and Panchkula. Its location on NH-7 has turned it into a natural extension of Chandigarh's real estate market, while still offering more spacious layouts and better per-square-foot value than the city core.

Vamana Arvindam sits directly on NH-7, Patiala Highway, just 12 minutes from Chandigarh International Airport — a 7.7-acre address positioned squarely in this growth path. For end-users, that means schools, hospitals, malls, and the airport are all a short drive away. For investors, it means the corridor's rental demand is driven by a steady mix of airport traffic, IT professionals working in Mohali and Panchkula, and families relocating from central Chandigarh in search of larger homes.
