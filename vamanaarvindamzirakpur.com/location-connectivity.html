Location & Connectivity

Vamana Arvindam sits directly on NH-7, Patiala Highway, Zirakpur — 12 minutes from Chandigarh International Airport, at the crossroads of Chandigarh, Mohali, and Panchkula.

Why This Address Matters

A Corridor Built for Access

Zirakpur's position on NH-7 has turned it into a natural extension of Chandigarh's real estate market, offering more spacious layouts and better per-square-foot value than the city core, without sacrificing access to it. Vamana Arvindam's address — High Ground Lights, Patiala Road — puts residents within a short drive of the airport, the Panchkula and Chandigarh IT corridors, and the Tricity's hospitals, schools, and malls.
